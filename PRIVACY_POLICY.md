# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

## Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to IssieBoard, IssieVoice and IssieCalc (collectively, "the Apps"), developed by the Technology Center at Beit Issie Shapiro. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the Apps handle information.

In short: everything you type, speak or calculate stays on your device. The Apps send only anonymous usage statistics, and feedback that you choose to send us.

## What Stays on Your Device

### IssieBoard (Keyboard)

IssieBoard is a custom keyboard. **Nothing you type is collected, stored or transmitted** — not to us and not to any third party. The keyboard itself does not access the network.

### IssieVoice (Communication App)

IssieVoice speaks typed text aloud. **Typed messages, spoken text and saved sentences stay on your device** and are never transmitted.

### IssieCalc (Calculator)

IssieCalc is a talking calculator. **Exercises, results and calculator memory stay on your device** and are never transmitted.

### Speech

Text-to-speech in IssieVoice and IssieCalc uses the voices built into your device's operating system.

## Information We Collect

### Anonymous Usage Statistics

The Apps use Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the Apps are used, so we can improve them. This includes:

- App opens and general usage events
- Device type, operating system version and app version
- Approximate country (derived by Google from the network connection)
- An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase

This data is **not** linked to your name, email or any account, does not include anything you type, speak or calculate, and is **not** used for advertising or tracking across other apps or websites.

### App Integrity Check

The Apps use Firebase App Check, which asks Apple (App Attest / DeviceCheck) or Google (Play Integrity) to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This exchange contains no personal information.

### Feedback You Send Us

The About screen lets you send us feedback. If you choose to do so, we receive:

- The title and text of your feedback
- Your email address, **only if you enter one** (optional), so we can reply
- The name of the App you sent it from

Feedback is sent to us through Google Firebase (Cloud Functions, hosted in the EU) and is used only to answer you and improve the Apps.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The Apps store settings locally on your device:

- **Keyboard and calculator configurations** — layouts, colors, visible keys and other customizations
- **Saved sentences** (IssieVoice only)
- **App settings** — voice, language, readout and display preferences

This data is stored using your device's standard storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android). It is not accessible to us. Like other app data, it may be included in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud or Google backup) if you have enabled them.

## Third-Party Services

The only third-party service the Apps use is **Google Firebase** (Analytics, App Check and Cloud Functions), as described above. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The Apps do **not**:

- Display advertisements or include advertising networks
- Sell or share data with anyone
- Track you across other companies' apps or websites
- Integrate with social media

## Permissions

The Apps do not request access to the camera, photos, microphone, contacts or location.

- **IssieBoard** asks for "Full Access" on iOS only so the keyboard can read the settings you make in the IssieBoard app (iOS shares them between the app and the keyboard only with Full Access). The keyboard does not use this access to send anything off your device.
- **IssieVoice** and **IssieCalc** use on-device text-to-speech.

## Children's Privacy

The Apps are designed for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage statistics are anonymous, and the optional email field in the feedback form is the only place personal information can be entered. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- **On your device:** delete the App (or clear its data on Android) to remove all its settings and saved content.
- **Usage statistics:** kept in aggregated form according to Firebase's retention settings, and not linked to you.
- **Feedback:** contact us to ask that feedback you sent, including your email, be deleted.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete personal information we hold about you. In practice, the only such information is feedback you chose to send. Contact us and we will help.
